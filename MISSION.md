# MadeBy — Mission, Vision & Values

*The foundation the rest of our work answers to. Read this before `ARCHITECTURE.md` (how it's built)
and `TESTING.md` (how we keep it honest). When those documents and this one disagree, this one wins.*

---

## Why we exist

More of the world's work — code first, everything else soon — is now made by people and machines
together. As that mix grows, one plain question gets harder to answer and more important to ask:
**who made this?**

Today the honest answer is usually *nobody knows*. Authorship is guessed, assumed, or lost. That
vacuum gets filled badly — by tools that accuse people of using AI, by credit that goes to the
loudest rather than the maker, by a slow erosion of trust in everything we read and run.

MadeBy exists to fill it well.

---

## Mission

**Make "who made it" an honest, verifiable part of the work itself — so trust, credit, and
accountability travel with what's created, between people and machines alike.**

We start with code, because that's where machines are writing the most, the fastest — and where a
wrong answer costs the most.

---

## Vision

A world where **"who made this?" always has an honest answer.**

Every piece of work carries a truthful, portable record of how it came to be — human, machine, or
both — kept by its makers, checkable by anyone, owned by no one. Disclosure is simply how things are
done: not a burden, not an accusation, just the norm. Provenance becomes a shared layer beneath
creative work — invisible when it works, and the thing everything else quietly stands on.

We don't get there by policing. We get there by making the honest thing the easy thing, and by being
the layer everyone — including their AI — chooses to build on.

---

## Values

Not aspirations — the constraints we have already chosen to live under, and the ones we will refuse
to trade away for growth.

**1. Disclosure, not detection.**
We report what a maker *states* about their work. We never guess whether something is AI, and we
never accuse. No detector, ever — because a false accusation is worse than the problem it claims to
solve, and no honest method to make one exists. The maker speaks; we keep the record.

**2. Honest by construction.**
We say only what the evidence supports. Past that, the answer is *unknown* — never a flattering guess
dressed as a fact. When we are unsure, we fail toward under-claiming, never toward a false verdict.
An honest gap, shown plainly, beats a confident lie.

**3. Evidence sets the ceiling.**
Every claim carries exactly the weight its evidence earns — and no more. A signature outranks a
say-so; a sworn statement outranks a signature. Nothing is presented as more certain than it is, and
no bug of ours can quietly promote a claim above its proof.

**4. Both sides, equal.**
Human and machine authorship ride the same ladder and get the same respect. We are pro-transparency,
not anti-AI. A person's handwritten work and an agent's disclosed work are both honorable, both worth
crediting, and neither is the shameful one. Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence — *made by
hi, made by ai.*

**5. The person is never the target.**
No shame pages. No surveillance. No scoring of people. We hold claims about *work*, not dossiers
about *people* — and where a real identity is involved, we point to where it already lives rather
than hoard it. Accountability, never a pillory.

**6. Open, and no lock-in.**
The standard is open for anyone to implement; the tools run in your own environment; the shared
record is a public good. Nothing you disclose is held hostage. We earn our keep by making provenance
resolvable across the world — not by trapping your data — because a trust layer only works if you
could walk away and it would still tell the truth.

**7. Take away until it's clear.**
The fewer moving parts, the more a trust tool can be trusted. We name things by what they are —
*made by ___* — and cut anything that has not earned its place. A product like ours is finished not
when there is nothing left to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.

---

*When a decision is hard and the values seem to pull against each other, the tie-breakers, in order:
**be honest, be fair, be clear.***
